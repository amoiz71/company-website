ABOUT OUR COMPANY

Your Trusted Partner in Textile Quality Assurance

Meticulous on-site evaluation of fabric specifications and finished apparel

At Eagle Eye Inspection Company, we understand the importance of quality, consistency, and reliability in the textile supply chain. Our inspection and audit services help businesses evaluate product quality, identify manufacturing issues, and improve confidence in their sourcing and production processes.

Our approach focuses on careful observation, systematic inspection, clear communication, and practical reporting to support better quality decisions. Whether you are dealing with woven fabrics, knitwear, denim, or finished garments, we serve as your vigilant eyes directly on the production floor.

Company Mission

To provide textile buyers, sourcing offices, and manufacturers with impartial, accurate, and transparent inspection findings, safeguarding product integrity and minimizing supply chain shipment risks.

Our Commitment to Quality

  • Uncompromising Independence: Objective evaluations that reflect true production quality.
  • Systematic Checklists: Thorough verification against agreed buyer tolerances and criteria.
  • Transparent Reporting: Detailed observations backed by clear photographic documentation.
  • Timely Communication: Fast turnaround on findings so production delays are avoided.
QUALITY INSPECTION & AUDITS

Comprehensive Textile Inspection Services

Tailored inspection solutions across every stage of the textile manufacturing cycle, from raw fabric to final carton dispatch.

Material Quality

1. Fabric Inspection

Inspect fabric for visible defects, shade variation, weaving issues, holes, stains, and other quality concerns according to the agreed inspection criteria.

  • Weaving & knitting defect classification
  • Shade variation & roll-to-roll consistency
  • Fabric width, weight & length verification
Workmanship

2. Garment Inspection

Examine finished garments for stitching quality, measurements, appearance, workmanship, labeling, and packaging requirements.

  • Specification sheet measurement audit
  • Seam strength, puckering & loose threads
  • Label accuracy, barcodes & hangtags
In-Process Control

3. Inline Inspection

Assess product quality during manufacturing to help identify potential issues before production progresses further.

  • Early detection of recurring sewing flaws
  • Verification against approved pre-production samples
  • Mitigating batch scrap and costly rework
Pre-Shipment FRI

4. Final Random Inspection

Evaluate a sample of finished goods before shipment using agreed sampling procedures and acceptance criteria.

  • Statistical sampling on 100% finished / packed lots
  • Critical, major, and minor defect count
  • Shipment authorization decision baseline
Facility Assessment

5. Textile Factory Audit

Support factory assessments covering production processes, quality management practices, workplace conditions, and operational procedures within the agreed audit scope.

  • Production capacity & machinery evaluation
  • Internal quality control system review
  • Workplace conditions & operational safety check
Dispatch Readiness

6. Packing & Shipment Inspection

Check packaging, carton markings, labeling, quantity, assortment, and shipment readiness against the provided requirements.

  • Carton drop test & structural durability
  • Shipping marks, barcodes & polybag warnings
  • Quantity tally & size/color assortment verification
STRUCTURED METHODOLOGY

Our Inspection Process

A systematic five-step approach delivering transparency, accuracy, and actionable quality findings.

01

Request and Requirements

The client shares product details, inspection requirements, specifications, and target dates.

02

Inspection Planning

The inspection scope, checklist, sampling requirements, and schedule are agreed.

03

On-Site Inspection

The inspector examines the relevant products, materials, records, or processes according to the agreed scope.

04

Findings and Reporting

Observations, defects, photographs, and results are documented clearly in an actionable report.

05

Quality Decision Support

The client receives findings to support decisions regarding corrective action, product acceptance, or shipment readiness.

SECTOR EXPERTISE

Industries We Serve

Specialized inspection capabilities tailored to the unique quality standards of distinct textile sub-sectors.

Garment Manufacturing

Quality checks across woven and knitted ready-made garments, assessing seam integrity, pattern symmetry, dimensions, and finishing quality.

Woven Fabrics

Inspection for shirting, sheeting, twill, poplin, and canvas fabrics focusing on yarn defects, weft bars, tensile structure, and color consistency.

Knitted Fabrics

Evaluation of single jersey, rib, interlock, fleece, and pique knit materials for drop stitches, spirality, fabric weight (GSM), and hand-feel.

Home Textiles

Quality verification for bed linens, towels, blankets, curtains, and kitchen textiles, ensuring hem neatness, sizing, and wash durability.

Denim and Casual Wear

Specialized assessment for denim trousers, jackets, and casuals checking wash effects, rivet and button fastening, whisker shading, and shrinkage.

Textile Exporters and Sourcing Companies

Impartial third-party support for international buyers, trading houses, and exporters verifying order readiness prior to port dispatch.
